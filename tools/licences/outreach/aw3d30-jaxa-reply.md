## What it answers

The letter asked one question: Research Data clause 2.3 permits commercial use
subject to advance **notification**, while the JAXA Site Policy — which clause
2.3's own preamble says the user must also follow — prohibits commercial use
without prior **permission**. Notification and permission are different
obligations, and only one can be discharged unilaterally.

The reply resolves it on scope rather than on precedence: **the Site Policy
governs unaltered individual images hosted on the JAXA website, not the AW3D30
dataset.** This product uses the dataset, resampled and reprojected — neither an
individual image nor unaltered — so the Site Policy's commercial prohibition does
not reach it, and the Research Data terms govern alone.

It also states that the notification obligation is satisfied by this exchange:
*"no further action is required on your part besides receiving this email."*

**What it does not do:** the notification named versions 3.1 and 4.1 and two
specific 4 km areas. Nothing in the reply says a fresh notification is never
needed again. Treat a materially wider commercial use — other AW3D30 versions,
or a substantially different scope — as requiring a new notification rather than
reading this as a blanket permission. Clause 2.5 also lets JAXA change the terms
without notice, so the register's verification date on the AW3D30 entries is what
keeps this from silently ageing.
